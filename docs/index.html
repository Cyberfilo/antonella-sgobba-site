Who I am

I am a Milan-based luxury hospitality executive with more than 30 years in hotels and travel. I am Group Director of Commercial Strategy at Italian Hospitality Collection. Before that I built the hospitality division of Itaca Luxury Collection, led Lindbergh Hotels & Resorts as Chief Commercial Officer, and directed sales and marketing at Hotel Principe di Savoia, Dorchester Collection.
